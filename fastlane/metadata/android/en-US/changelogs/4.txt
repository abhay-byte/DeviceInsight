DeviceInsight 1.0.3 (Build 4) — CALIPER Calibration, Camera & Thermal Integrity

This is a major calibration and data-integrity update. Every first-launch permission now follows CALIPER (Paper drafting language) and never auto-pops the system dialog. Camera inventory is deduped and thermal bogus zones are filtered. All calibration sheets are now warm Paper.

COVER & FIRST-LAUNCH
• New 00 · Device Insights cover: 96dp paper square, 2dp radius, ink crosshair with 3dp accent dot and 24dp inner hairline, Device/Insights serif 44sp, DEVICE INSIGHTS mono caps, INSTRUMENT·CALIPER REV A panel and BEGIN CALIBRATION. First thing on fresh install.
• Entire calibration forced to Paper (#F4F1E8/#FBF9F3) — previously only 05 MEDIA was light while 01-04 were Carbon on dark system. Now 00 cover, 01 USAGE, 02 CAMERA, 03 OVERLAY, 04 ROOT and certificate are all Paper with graph-paper grid.
• Fixed first-launch gate: LaunchedEffect now watches caliperMigratedFlow so fresh installs correctly show calibration instead of staying on Overview.
• Media step removed: 05 MEDIA not used per feedback. Defaults to PAPER persisted on entry/finish. 4 numbered sheets (01-04) + cover + certificate, progress 00·✓ 01·✓ 02·✓ 03·✓ 04·✓.

PERMISSIONS — NEVER AUTO, ALWAYS EXPLICIT
• Removed MainActivity auto CAMERA request. System purple dialog now only on explicit HardKey tap.
• 01 USAGE now actually grants: AppOpsManager check, FIG.1 USAGE PATH LOCKED/GRANTED with dotted hatch, GRANT USAGE ACCESS → ACTION_USAGE_ACCESS_SETTINGS and onResume re-check, CONTINUE→CAMERA when granted. SKIP shows ≈ estimates.
• 02 CAMERA CALIPER gate: RequestPermission only on GRANT CAMERA tap, FIG.1 hatch preview, NOTE 002 rationale, permanent-deny → OPEN SYSTEM SETTINGS. No auto popup.
• 03 OVERLAY now actually asks: Settings.canDrawOverlays check, FIG.2 HUD OVERLAY hatch, GRANT OVERLAY → ACTION_MANAGE_OVERLAY_PERMISSION (package: URI) with onResume re-check, CONTINUE→ROOT when granted.
• Settings → 02 PERMISSIONS small card (only when CAMERA denied) with 12dp dotted hatch, NOTE 002, GRANT CAMERA and OPEN SETTINGS. Only place that opens the system popup.
• DevicesTab CHANNEL LOCKED hatch with GRANT/OPEN SETTINGS and auto-reload via HardwareScreen onCameraGrantedReload.

CAMERA — 10 → 4
• RMX1931 HAL reports 10 devices (5× 4608×3456 same sensor, 2× 3264×2448 etc.). Added dedup by facing|maxSize|focal (e.g. 1|4608x3456|5.58) keeping first physical module. Logs dedup key for diagnostics.
• Filtered tiny back auxiliary depth/macro <2 MP (1600×1200 1.9MP depth on RMX1931) — user expects 3 back + 1 front = 4, not 10. Front kept even if small. Count now MISC 4 cameras·2 USB, CAMERAS·4, sequential names without gaps. Provider gated: emptyList() when CAMERA not granted → CHANNEL LOCKED hatch.

THERMAL — 596°C/-274°C → Real Silicon
• ThermalProvider now filters temp in -20..110°C (real phone silicon). RMX1931 had 72 zones with -274 (absolute zero) and 588-618°C pa-therm2 spikes → 596°C PEAK / -274°C MIN / 41°C AVG and false CRITICAL. Now 65 sensors, PEAK 92°C (pa-therm1), AVG 49°C, MIN 4°C, 92.0°C throttling zone, CPU 52°C ramp honest. Logs filter bogus $type=$temp raw=$tempRaw.
